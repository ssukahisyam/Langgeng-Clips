# Privacy Policy Disclosures

Minimum disclosures for Phase 3 monetization and analytics:

- The app uses a user-provided Groq API key for transcription and AI features.
- The app may use Sentry for crash reporting with PII disabled and secret/path scrubbing.
- The app may use PostHog-style product analytics events without file paths, transcript text, or API keys.
- Free tier may add a `Made with Langgeng Clip` watermark.
- Future AdMob integration may show banner, rewarded, or low-frequency interstitial ads.
- Future Play Billing integration may process subscriptions and free trials through Google Play.
